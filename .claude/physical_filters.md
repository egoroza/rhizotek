### By Spore-Bearing Surface (Hymenophore)

- **Gilled (Agarics):** Mushrooms with blade-like gills under the cap (e.g., _Psilocybe cubensis_, _Pleurotus_, _Lentinula edodes_).
- **Pored (Polypores & Boletes):** Spongy or woody undersides with tiny holes (e.g., _Ganoderma lucidum_, _Trametes versicolor_, _Boletus edulis_).
- **Toothed (Hydnoid):** Spines or icicle-like structures (e.g., _Hericium erinaceus_).
- **Other Forms:** Corals, jellies, puffballs, and cup fungi.

### By Ecology & Primary Substrate (Where they grow)

- **Wood-Decay (Primary Saprotrophs):** Found on living trees, dead logs, or stumps (translates to hardwood sawdust/master's mix).
- **Soil & Litter (Secondary Saprotrophs):** Found in leaf litter, grass, or garden beds (translates to compost, straw, or coco coir bulk substrates)[cite: 2].
- **Dung (Coprophilous):** Found on herbivore manure in pastures (translates to manure-based bulk substrates).
- **Tree Symbionts (Mycorrhizal):** Found growing from tree roots in forests (triggers the uncultivatable application warning)[cite: 2].

### By Cap & Stem Morphology

- **Distinct Cap & Central Stem:** Classic mushroom shape (e.g., _Agaricus bisporus_).
- **Off-Center or Missing Stem (Pleurotoid):** Shelf-like growth from the side of wood (e.g., Oysters).
- **Sessile / Bracket Fungi:** Woody conks with no stem attached directly to the host (e.g., Reishi).
- **Distinctive Veils:** Presence of a universal veil (volva at the base) or partial veil (annulus/ring on the stem).

### By Unique Traits & Identification Markers

- **Spore Print Color:** White, pink, brown, purple-black, or black[cite: 2].
- **Chemical Reactions:** Bruising, color changes when cut, or latex secretions[cite: 2].
- **Hygrophanous Caps:** Caps that change color dramatically as they dry out.

### By Application Status (Cultivation Difficulty)

- **Beginner:** Highly aggressive mycelium, resistant to contamination[cite: 2].
- **Intermediate:** Requires specific temperature drops or precise humidity to fruit[cite: 2].
- **Expert:** Requires specialized cold-shocking, burying blocks, or specific supplementation[cite: 2].
